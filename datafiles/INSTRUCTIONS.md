*-- Keybinds --*

**\[ESC]** : Open/close main menu

**\[CTRL] + S** : Save opened chart

**\[CTRL] + O** : Import file

**\[CTRL] + E** : Export opened chart as coordinate sheets

**\[CTRL] + N** : Open new blank chart



*-- Settings --*

**Pause music in menu** - When toggled, music will not play when the main menu is opened.

**Toggle metronome** - When toggles, music will be replaced with a metronome, playing on the \& of every beat.

**Color marchers** - Marchers (excluding Colorguard and Majorette) will be color-coded according to their instruments.

**Label marchers** - All marchers are drawn to the screen with a label showing their number and symbol.
